Tomato Beef Clay Pot — comic cover poster, v3.

UNIFIED COMIC STYLE (applies to EVERYTHING, ingredients included): playful manga / comic illustration with bold clean dark-brown outlines, simplified cel-shaded shapes, slight hand-painted texture, cheerful exaggerated forms. NOTHING photorealistic. The four reference colors are combined into one cohesive visual language (backgrounds, banners, props, steam, decorations): sunny yellow #F2B33D, sage green #A9C47F, lemon yellow #F7E14B, warm orange #E8751A, on warm cream #FFF6E0. Do NOT paste literal paint swatches; use the colors as flat comic color blocks and accents.

INGREDIENT HUES stay true to real food but rendered in the same comic style: tomatoes vivid red, thin wavy hot-pot beef rolls light rosy gray-brown with pale fat edges (NOT steak), enoki creamy white, bok choy fresh green with pale stems, dried tofu flat pale cream-yellow ribbons, scallion bright green, broth rich red-orange, clay pot charcoal black.

LAYOUT (top → bottom, 3:4 portrait):
- TOP: oversized bold hand-lettered Chinese title "番茄肥牛砂锅" on a lemon-yellow rounded banner, dark-brown characters, slightly wobbly comic lettering.
- Under title: one small line of Chinese text "肥牛 · 西红柿 · 干豆腐 · 金针菇 · 油菜".
- MIDDLE (main area): comic black earthenware clay pot centered, bubbling red-orange tomato broth, draped thin wavy beef roll slices, red tomato chunks, white enoki bundle, green bok choy, pale-yellow tofu ribbons, chopped scallion; exaggerated comic steam swirls in cream/orange; small sage-green and orange sparkle doodles.
- BOTTOM: three rounded step chips in one row, numbered, short Chinese text:
  chip 1 (sunny yellow): "1 番茄去皮切丁"
  chip 2 (sage green): "2 肥牛开水焯"
  chip 3 (warm orange): "3 炒沙再咕嘟"

TEXT RULES: render EXACTLY the Chinese strings above, large and prominent, handwritten-style comic font, dark-brown ink; no other text, no English, no hex codes, no watermark, no human figures.

Clean composition, generous margins, warm cream background with subtle flat comic color-block corners.
